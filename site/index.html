How current is this? The site updates itself every morning at about 6 a.m. Eastern. Anything the Clerk of Council posts to Legistar (agendas for upcoming meetings, minutes, roll-call votes) shows up here the next morning. Agendas appear before a meeting; minutes and votes appear only once the Clerk publishes them, which is often some time after the meeting. The time of the latest update is at the bottom of this page. Source: City of Cincinnati Legistar public data (cincinnatioh.legistar.com). This is an independent project, not an official City record or website. The Clerk of Council keeps the official minutes. Explanations of how Council works are adapted from WVXU's guides: How to understand Council legislation and agendas and How to keep up with Cincinnati government. Topic and department tags come from keyword lists, so treat the trend numbers as a guide, not an exact count.
