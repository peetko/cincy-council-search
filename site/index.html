How City Hall works

How an idea becomes law in Cincinnati, and what the record shows about the process: how long it takes, what stalls, who lobbies, and how the workload has changed.

  1. 1. IntroducedThe City Manager, the Mayor or Council members file legislation with the Clerk of Council.
  2. 2. CommitteeA Council committee hears it, asks questions, and recommends whether to pass it.
  3. 3. Full CouncilCouncil votes, usually on Wednesdays. Five of nine votes pass an ordinance; an emergency clause needs six.
  4. 4. MayorThe Mayor signs it, or vetoes it (Council can override with six votes).
  5. 5. Takes effect30 days later, or right away if it passed as an emergency.
